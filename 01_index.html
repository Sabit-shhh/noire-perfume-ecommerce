The Noire atelier

🧪

Source & Select

Our perfumers travel the world to forge direct relationships with the growers of the rarest raw materials. No intermediaries. No compromise on origin.

02
⚗️

Compose & Age

A formula may undergo hundreds of iterations over years before it is deemed ready. Our fragrances are aged in glass for a minimum of six months before bottling.

03
🫙

Fill & Seal

Each bottle is filled by hand, sealed with our signature gold collar, and engraved with a unique batch number. Limited production ensures uncompromising quality.
